The quantum threat to cybersecurity is fundamentally different from most traditional technology risks. The challenge is not simply what happens when such a machine exists — the challenge is preparing before it exists.

PQC Readiness Assessment

Enterprise cryptographic environments are complex, distributed, and deeply embedded across applications, infrastructure, devices, vendors, and third-party systems. Replacing vulnerable cryptography can therefore take years.

Our PQC Readiness Assessment helps organizations understand their current cryptographic exposure and establish a practical roadmap toward a post-quantum-ready environment.

Assessment Areas

Cryptographic Discovery

Identify where cryptography is deployed throughout the enterprise.

Algorithm Assessment

Understand the algorithms, protocols, certificates, and implementations currently in use.

Data Sensitivity & Longevity

Identify information that must remain confidential for many years and may face greater exposure.

Dependency Analysis

Map dependencies across applications, cloud environments, vendors, partners, and legacy systems.

Migration Complexity

Evaluate the effort required to transition vulnerable systems toward post-quantum standards.

Prioritization

Rank systems according to business criticality, cryptographic exposure, and migration complexity.

Business Outcome

The objective is not simply to tell an organization: "You need post-quantum cryptography."

The objective is to answer: "What do we need to change, where do we need to change it, how difficult will it be, and what should we do first?"
